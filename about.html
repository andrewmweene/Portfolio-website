Hello! I'm Andrew Buumba Mweene, a passionate Software Engineering student from Zambia building the future, one line of code at a time.

My journey

From taking things apart to building them.

I grew up in Chibombo, Zambia, where curiosity about how things work led me to take apart radios and old computers as a kid. That curiosity turned into passion when I discovered programming.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Science at The University of Zambia, I am specializing in Software Engineering because I believe great software can solve real problems, from improving education access to helping local businesses grow.

When I am not coding, you will find me reading tech blogs, playing football with friends, or teaching basic computer skills to kids in my community.
