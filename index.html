About me & skills

Curious by nature.
Useful by design.

I grew up in Chibombo, Zambia, where curiosity about how things work led me to take apart radios and old computers. That curiosity became a passion for programming and the ability to create something real from pure logic.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Science at The University of Zambia, specializing in Software Engineering. I believe great software can solve real problems, from improving education access to helping local businesses grow.

When I am not coding, I read tech blogs, play football with friends, or teach basic computer skills to kids in my community.
